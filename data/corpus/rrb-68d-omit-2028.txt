Reserve Bank of India (Regional Rural Banks – Income Recognition, Asset Classification and Provisioning) Fourth Amendment Directions, 2028
Please refer to the Reserve Bank of India (Regional Rural Banks – Income Recognition, Asset Classification and Provisioning) Second Amendment Directions, 2026 dated July 16, 2026.
